# Group Dinner Planner v1 — normative contract

Version 1.4.0, 2026-11-02. This document overrides background briefs and draft prompts. MUST denotes required application behavior. Requirement IDs appear in executable Vitest test names and `specs/`. Fixtures are test/rehearsal data only.

## Scope and setup

- **SET-01** A private Telegram organizer can organize up to five active dinners by default, each for 2–6 intended attendees, including themselves, in one destination city. A Mini App form collects city, optional neighborhood (blank means citywide), a date window, party size and one required per-person budget. The organizer owns timezone, currency and duration. Device timezone, USD, 90 minutes, two people and the next two weeks are visible, editable defaults; saving explicitly confirms timezone/currency and consent. Guests inherit these shared details. Names are obtained from Telegram upon joining; no guest names are required upfront. Windows are bounded to 31 days. The trial city MUST NOT be hardcoded.
- **SET-02** Dates with missing zones, ambiguous relative wording, invalid local times, or DST ambiguity MUST be clarified; the system MUST NOT silently choose an interpretation. All intervals are stored as offset-bearing instants; local display uses the dinner timezone. Revisions are allowed only before the active dinner starts.

## Identity and privacy

- **JOIN-01** Dinner invitations are unguessable, dinner-specific and expire. Starting the bot with an invitation grants neither consent nor membership. Each person must explicitly consent to Muse processing of their input and sharing of plan details (attendees, venue, time, duration, cost and reservation handoff). The organizer must approve each joining membership. All approved members, including the organizer, are required by default.
- **AUTH-01** Every action MUST authenticate the private Telegram sender, bind to the correct dinner, verify consent/approved role and expected state revision, and reject unauthorized actions. Display names, usernames, model instructions and forwarded messages confer no authority. Group-chat updates MUST NOT initiate dinner processing.
- **PRIV-01** Unshared requirements, raw messages, calendar busy intervals and OAuth credentials MUST NOT appear in another member's context or shared summaries. A participant may separately opt in to sharing the exact reviewed manual form answers with approved dinner members under SHARE-01. Muse extraction gets the requesting member's input and constraints; research gets anonymous aggregated requirements and authorized setup. Public summaries use fixed application projections, never unreviewed model prose. Operational logs exclude request bodies, query strings, tokens and conversations.
- **PRIV-02** `/stop` withdraws participation across all dinners and cancels pending communication; required membership MUST NOT silently disappear. `/delete` removes the requesting user's constraints, consent, credentials and pending private work; affected dinners remain blocked until an explicit membership decision. Deleting the organizer deletes the dinner. Dinner-scoped data expires seven days after dinner end/window end. Privacy, support and disconnect commands MUST be available before joining.

## Collection, research and feasibility

- **CON-01** Manual availability supports the entire workflow. Patch updates preserve unaffected requirements and record firm versus flexible input. Explicit willingness is mandatory; free calendar slots alone do not establish willingness.
- **CAL-01** Optional Google access requests only primary-calendar free/busy. OAuth state is random, expiring, single-use and bound to the requesting member/dinner. Token credentials are encrypted with authenticated encryption. A browser callback only stages credentials: the requesting Telegram identity must activate the connection. Revocation and disconnect remove access; unavailable calendars are reported honestly and manual input continues.
- **RES-01** Live Muse web search discovers venues within the selected area. Each claim has a source URL, retrieval time and supporting evidence. Price, budget basis, opening, dietary, travel and reservation claims are distinct. Missing, stale or contradictory support remains unresolved. Cuisine is not dietary evidence. The runtime MUST never fabricate availability or claim a reservation exists.
- **FEAS-01** Deterministic code checks required attendance, explicit availability, known calendar conflicts, duration, area, budget basis, firm dietary requirements and travel limits. Unknown firm constraints block agreement. Flexible preferences may influence recommendations but MUST NOT silently become firm constraints or erase firm requirements.
- **DIET-01** Every firm dietary requirement needs specific venue evidence. Contradictory sources require resolution. An allergy remains provisional until that participant explicitly reports confirmation from restaurant staff for the proposed venue; another participant or the model cannot attest on their behalf. Their affirmative staff report is private evidence for their own allergy and may resolve a missing website claim, while contradictory or known negative sources still block agreement.

## Proposals and agreement

- **PROP-01** Present at most two researched options and recommend one active proposal. Choosing an alternative is an organizer action that creates a new active version. Every proposal snapshot is immutable.
- **REV-01** Material changes to time, venue, attendees, duration or price basis create a fresh proposal version and clear all confirmations. Constraint changes recheck feasibility and invalidate agreement. Historical buttons cannot confirm the new version. Existing reservations are never automatically modified.
- **MED-01** Objections are clarified privately. Preserve firm requirements; seek explicit willingness before relaxing flexible input. Permit at most two revised rounds per open disagreement, then escalate to the organizer. An unresolved required objection blocks agreement. A schedule change initiates research/replanning rather than retaining agreement.
- **AGR-01** Agreement requires every required approved participant to explicitly confirm the current feasible version. Silence, messages sent, a free calendar, tentative responses and model-generated decisions never count. Concurrency MUST NOT allow a confirmation to race past a material revision.
- **FIN-01** An agreed plan provides a correct `.ics` event with stable UID, version/sequence, UTC instants and escaped/folded text, plus a sourced reservation link or explicit manual handoff. Booking status always distinguishes handoff from an actual booking. No bookings, payments, calendar writes, automated restaurant contact or travel booking in v1.

## Reliability and communication

- **OPS-01** Authenticate Telegram webhooks with a secret header; durably deduplicate update IDs. Atomically persist dinner state, inbox completion and outbound work. Use transactional state version checks. Recover pending work after restart. Outbound network ambiguity MUST be recorded and MUST NOT trigger blind resend; successful delivery is not agreement.
- **OPS-02** Notify the organizer at setup, collection completion, proposals, agreement and blockers. Shared updates contain aggregate progress only. Reminders require opt-in, at most once per participant per dinner-local date, and only 09:00–20:59 local time. Recheck delivery eligibility at send time. Rate and usage limits are configurable.
- **EVAL-01** Versioned Muse evaluations cover extraction, follow-ups, disagreement, dietary search, privacy probes and prompt injection. Evaluate structured decisions and source grounding, not exact wording. Critical authorization/privacy failures block release. Live checks and real-group trial are distinct from deterministic fixtures.

## Telegram Mini App

- **UI-01** `/start`, `/new`, invitation links and the bot menu open the same mobile planner at `/app/`. The normal flow MUST NOT require typed commands, ISO dates, IANA identifiers, JSON or assigned-name selection. Existing dinners, commands and in-progress setup drafts remain usable.
- **UI-02** Each person privately selects their own willing dates and time ranges; bulk applying hours is allowed, preselecting willing dates is not. Food input requires an explicit choice of no requirements or entered requirements. Travel limits, personal budget and flexible preferences are optional advanced inputs. DST gaps and ambiguous wall times require correction or an explicit offset choice.
- **UI-03** A guest explicitly consents and submits their response once. Pending responses are private and excluded from planning until organizer approval atomically claims a place. Only the requesting user can read their own pending answers. The organizer sees pending names, never private answers.
- **UI-04** Mini App APIs validate Telegram-signed `initData` server-side (maximum age one hour, future skew at most 30 seconds), reject duplicate parameters and require server-recorded private bot contact. Actor IDs, roles and membership are never accepted from client assertions. APIs project only authorized plan details, own private answers and aggregate progress; authentication material is not stored in browser persistence or operational logs.
- **UI-05** Each mutation has a persistent idempotency key and executes with state/outbox updates in one transaction. Form freshness binds shared settings, own inputs and own membership, allowing unrelated people to save concurrently. Research uses the latest complete planning inputs; obsolete queued tasks/results are discarded quietly. Chat contains meaningful milestones/blockers, while the dashboard owns field feedback and progress.
- **UI-06** The dashboard supports membership approval, own response edits, organizer shared-detail edits, research, at most two proposals, alternative selection, version-bound confirmation, objections, own objection resolution, explicit allergy staff reports, `.ics` export, reservation handoff, Google connection/activation/disconnect, opt-in reminders, stop and deletion. Unresolved private evidence is shown only to its owner; aggregate feasibility can be shared.

- **UI-07** Phone layouts MUST stack paired native date/time controls below 600px and prevent overlap or horizontal overflow, including iPhone WebKit. Date-window labels are “Earliest date” and “Latest date”. The header identifies the product as “Dinner Planner” with “Powered by Muse” attribution. The shared budget field is labeled “Budget per person”; legacy total budgets MUST NOT be silently relabeled or reinterpreted.
- **UI-08** Availability uses one editor: choose willing dates, specify “I'm free from” / “I need to leave by”, then apply. The UI explains that dinner must fit within those available hours. Saved ranges are summaries with Edit/Remove; unapplied changes must be applied or discarded before final submission. Multiple ranges, overnight availability, timezone and explicit per-date DST choices remain supported.
- **CANCEL-01** Only the currently consenting organizer can explicitly confirm whole-dinner cancellation before the dinner begins, including after agreement. Cancellation is terminal for that dinner: status is `cancelled`, the end timestamp starts seven-day retention, current proposal/confirmations clear, and immutable history remains. Existing invitations and pending OAuth links are invalidated; local dinner-scoped calendar credentials and staged pending answers are removed. Pending request metadata remains sufficient for private cancellation views and notices. No future planning, joining, confirmation, activation or calendar export is allowed. Each consenting approved/pending attendee gets one durable cancellation notice; repeat actions do not reset retention or duplicate notices. Obsolete queued work is suppressed; in-flight provider results cannot revive the dinner. Stop, disconnect and deletion remain privacy actions and cannot reopen it. Cancellation frees the organizer to create a new dinner and does not modify reservations or previously downloaded calendars.

- **SHARE-01** Manual form answers (availability, dietary requirements, preferences, personal budget and travel including starting point) remain private by default. Sharing requires the author's explicit choice and exact answer preview, separately from processing/calendar consent. Only currently approved, consenting dinner members can see another approved member's opted-in answers. Pending answers, calendar busy data, credentials, raw chats and staff reports never enter shared responses. Old-client submissions without an explicit sharing choice revoke visibility of edited answers. A grant must match the effective reviewed answer snapshot; inherited or retained unseen fields stay private. Enabling binds the author's current form version; disabling is immediate even after cancellation/end. Stop/delete removes grants, and receipt replay cannot restore revoked sharing. Sharing-only changes neither invalidate confirmations nor rerun research. Research model context remains minimized regardless of sharing.
- **RES-02** Every research attempt has a persisted, dinner-scoped safe lifecycle and support reference. Approved members see basic status; only the organizer sees typed diagnostics, stage, timestamp, safe HTTP/request identifiers, counts and guidance. Raw provider payloads, private inputs and credentials are excluded from diagnostic storage/logs. Distinguish provider/configuration, timeout/network, structured-output/search failures and completed searches with no suitable options or unresolved evidence. Use provider-supported tool settings while requiring proof of completed live search; unsupported claims remain unresolved. Retries are manual. Stale/cancelled/stopped/exhausted work cannot leave misleading active status or overwrite newer attempts. Attempt data follows dinner retention/deletion.

## Release acceptance

The defining demo is: four consenting attendees join privately; Muse researches two sourced choices; a dietary objection and a schedule change force a suitable alternative; all required people freshly confirm the same version; an accurate calendar export and reservation handoff follow. A real-group trial, working credentials, Google publication review and official submission requirements are release gates, never implied by passing fixture tests. Scope reductions require an explicit user decision.

- **MULTI-01** The organizer limit is configurable separately from service capacity and enforced atomically in every creation path. Active means no end timestamp, including agreed or consent-blocked dinners. Joining someone else's dinner does not consume an organizer slot. Cancelling or normal completion frees a slot. Optional shared names are trimmed, at most 80 characters, and default to “Dinner in {city}”. Only the consenting organizer can rename an ongoing dinner against the current form version; naming is metadata and MUST NOT invalidate confirmations or trigger research.
- **MULTI-02** With multiple ongoing dinners, normal entry opens Your dinners; explicit dinner, invitation and create links retain their targets. Lists group Organizing, Joining, and Past or cancelled with stable date ordering. Switching requires authorized dinner access, updates chat focus explicitly, clears transient state and rejects late responses from previously selected dinners. Every mutation and async job remains dinner-scoped. Telegram updates identify their dinner; /resume without an ID offers explicit choices when multiple roles need consent. /stop and /delete remain account-wide and clearly say so. The existing global one-reminder-per-24-hours throttle remains shared across dinners.

## Coordination flow revision — normative override

This section supersedes approval-gated joining and evidence-gated group agreement above.

- Valid invitation possession plus authenticated processing consent admits a participant atomically within capacity. Members may join before answering. Resetting the link invalidates old invitations without removing members.
- Named manual availability has its own explicit sharing grant; previous private answers stay private. Calendar busy intervals never enter shared schedules. A declined grant does not prevent participation.
- No-overlap scheduling creates at most two rounds of explicit suggestions. Acceptance adds only the selected window; silence never adds availability. Stale or calendar-conflicting acceptance fails. Exhaustion calls for organizer date changes.
- Every required participant explicitly confirms the same restaurant/time version. Pending restaurant evidence permits group agreement; known conflicts and unresolved objections block it. Verification and reservation status are independent and prominently displayed.
- Selected-venue verification runs at most two focused passes without changing the restaurant or confirmations. Unsupported claims remain unresolved. Material plan changes still reset confirmations.
- Automatic bookings require approved provider access, a current fully verified unanimous plan, and explicit organizer authorization. No payments or card guarantees are supported. Ambiguous submission blocks duplicate bookings. Organizer-reported external bookings remain distinct from provider confirmations.
- Unsupported booking uses verified links or phone details and clear instructions. Existing bookings are not automatically cancelled or rebooked. Calendar export records group agreement without implying a reservation.

- **YELP-01** Optional Yelp Places discovery uses only public city/area and a generic restaurant query or allowlisted, positively expressed cuisine preferences. Queries must never include arbitrary private preference prose, health requirements, participant identity or travel origin. Discovery prioritizes the expressed cuisines with bounded, fair query merging; these remain flexible preferences. It supplies transient name/address search hints; Muse independently verifies identities and claims from non-Yelp sources before storing results. Yelp price tiers never become monetary amounts, dietary proof, or reservation confirmation. Yelp content remains in bounded memory for less than 24 hours; only business IDs and typed outcome codes enter durable discovery diagnostics. Failures retain independent web research with a visible organizer outcome. API keys remain server-side. Live use requires explicit production mode and an eligible account plan; trial evaluation never silently enables live dinner jobs. Focused verification of an existing venue must not trigger replacement discovery.

- **RES-03** Source verification must distinguish independently established restaurant identity from incomplete feasibility details. Identity requires cited, independently retrieved non-Yelp name, street address and city; raw completed search-tool title/snippet may establish identity when a safe direct fetch fails, but model-authored answer text never substitutes for retrieval. Snippet fallback must not override a successfully fetched conflicting page or an unsafe/excluded source, and must not mark price, hours or dietary claims verified; missing neighborhood corroboration must remain unresolved without deleting an otherwise identified suggestion. Unknown claims and unsupported directory content must be scrubbed before storage. Ordinary unknown details can accompany a group choice under the coordination override; known conflicts and booking verification gates remain unchanged. Missing facts appear in plain language with clear next steps rather than a generic failure wall. Organizer diagnostics retain only typed aggregate verification reason counts, with no provider prose or private inputs. Old attempts without this metadata must not invent reasons.

## Staged discovery and booking revision — normative override

- **SHORTLIST-01** Present up to three real restaurant choices. Yelp discovery fetches at most ten relevant candidates, with bounded detail/menu retrieval and a separate tool-free Muse ranking task. Publish real choices before model ranking; ranking failure retains application ordering. Persist progress and candidate IDs. Known closed restaurants are excluded; unavailable menu/price data stays visibly unconfirmed. An unknown numeric placeholder must never be displayed as a free meal or treated as verified pricing.
- **SHORTLIST-02** Yelp content lives only in an expiring UNLOGGED cache for less than24hours. Durable dinners, immutable proposals and jobs contain business IDs and application-authored placeholders; authorized views and calendar downloads hydrate fresh listing details without persisting the hydrated copy. Expired listing content is not served; current IDs are refreshed. Private dietary/travel URLs never become public identity sources.
- **SHORTLIST-03** Separate jobs handle short ranking and focused verification. A user's selection, confirmation or objection prevents automatic ranking changes. A material verified price change creates a newly announced version requiring fresh confirmations. Both API and Telegram confirmations require a fresh selected listing. Source failures retain current choices. Empty/failed directory discovery triggers bounded identity-only web discovery, with no invented options or silently relaxed firm requirements.
- **ASK-01** Organizers receive actionable, plain-language questions for schedule/search decisions and booking next steps. Members see safe progress and available choices; technical diagnostics stay behind organizer details. Fewer than three real matches must show the available choices and a request to adjust search scope, never fabricated restaurants.
- **BOOK-02** After unanimous agreement, the organizer may review the exact venue/date/time/party and authorize a booking with contact details. Yelp reservations require separately approved partner access, disabled by default. Existing verified-plan gates remain. No charges, deposits or card guarantees are supported; policies needing additional acceptance use a clear handoff. Recheck authorization before hold and booking, bind jobs to provider/version, never retry an ambiguous POST, and declare confirmation only with an actual provider reference. A live directory key alone is not reservation access. Without approved access, show a current listing/contact link and distinguish organizer-reported bookings.

- **SITE-01** Selected restaurant verification starts with its official website. If its URL is unknown, use one short identity-only lookup with actual search-result provenance. The original same-origin limits are superseded by SITE-02 and SITE-03 below; use location-associated sources and bounded targeted fallback research. Reject unsafe redirects and directory/social hosts. Missing facts remain unknown; a model timeout preserves independently verifiable site facts and existing choices. Never repeat the same selected-site check automatically. Organizer-provided URLs require current-version ownership, affect research staleness, and cannot silently change the selected restaurant or confirmations. Ask for a readable official site/menu when retrieval or identity checks fail.

## Booking destinations and targeted evidence

- **LINK-01** Every displayed restaurant may expose a sourced reservation destination to consenting dinner members before agreement. A published official reservation link may point to an external provider even when that provider blocks automated reading. A link never implies table availability or a completed booking. Official websites, listings and phone fallbacks have distinct labels; menu/evidence URLs are not booking destinations.
- **LINK-02** After agreement prominently show the selected restaurant, time, party size and booking destination. External booking is not gated on complete feasibility evidence. Preserve specific cautions and firm allergy requirements. Only an actual provider confirmation or explicit organizer report changes reservation status.
- **SITE-02** Associate facts across identity-matched official pages and relevant published external sources, using location sections rather than requiring every excerpt to repeat the full identity. Shared domains alone cannot establish branch-specific facts. Preserve contradictory sources and exact supporting excerpts. Unknown prices never become free meals, and recurring hours never establish reservation inventory.
- **SITE-03** Read at most six pages, 2 MB per page and 24,000 normalized characters within a ten-second fetch phase. Selected research has a 45-second extraction bound and 75-second overall deadline; one targeted search pass may use at most three missing-fact queries. Preserve partial links and facts. No automatic repetition of exhausted checks.
- **LINK-03** Enrich the three displayed choices through bounded database-backed work, with at most two restaurant checks in flight. Results are version/restaurant/source-bound, deduplicated and recoverable. Metadata cannot silently change selection or confirmations. Existing dinners remain readable with optional metadata.
- **DIAG-01** Distinguish retrieval failure, extraction timeout, invalid output, unsupported claims, branch ambiguity and absent information. Keep safe phase durations and model/provider identifiers behind organizer diagnostics, without page bodies, private prompts or raw errors. Ask an actionable organizer question when it can resolve a blocker.
