# Dinner aggregate

All commands are pure and return a replacement aggregate. The caller persists it with an optimistic revision check. Actors must come from verified transport identity; consent and staff reports must come from the participant's own interaction. Evidence is data supplied by a research adapter, never invented by this module. No command sends messages, books restaurants, or reads calendars.

## Requirements and verification

- SETUP-01: City, bounded area, local date window, valid timezone, confirmed timezone and currency, positive duration and budget, and 2–6 distinct intended attendees including the organizer are explicit.
- JOIN-01: Intended membership requires organizer approval, prior bot start and active dinner consent. All approved participants default to required. An organizer must explicitly change a member's required flag before removing that attendee from the intended list. The organizer stays required.
- CONSTRAINT-01: Constraint patches preserve other fields. Manual availability and optional calendar busy intervals use explicit ISO offsets; positive intervals are required. Firm and flexible diet, budget and travel constraints remain distinct. Preferences never substitute for evidence.
- DIETARY-01: Dietary patches merge by requirement and preserve prior requirements, including when an empty array is submitted. An existing allergy or firm requirement cannot be weakened through an ordinary patch. `removeDietary` requires the authenticated affected participant and explicitly deletes that requirement, its staff reports and all proposal confirmations.
- PRICE-01: Source price evidence must include a currency equal to the setup currency. Missing or mismatching currencies remain unresolved; amounts are never compared across currencies.
- VENUE-01: Venue IDs must be unique in research updates, persisted dinners and proposal snapshots. A duplicate ID cannot select one venue for feasibility while inheriting another venue's participant reports.
- CONSTRAINT-03: Undefined patch values preserve existing constraints. A patch cannot weaken an existing firm budget or travel limit to flexible. `removeFirmLimit(dinner, authenticatedMemberId, "budget" | "travel", now)` explicitly removes that participant's selected limit and invalidates the current proposal and confirmations; the application must obtain an explicit decision first.
- MEMBER-02: An explicit organizer decision making a participant optional closes that participant's open objections, retaining their history. Remaining required participants may then confirm a new feasible proposal.
- TIME-02: `parseLocalDateTime(local, timezone, offsetMinutes?)` converts a wall-clock date and time to an explicit offset ISO timestamp. It rejects nonexistent DST times and requires a matching explicit offset for repeated DST hours. Availability and proposal schemas accept only explicit offset timestamps.
- CALENDAR-01: A connected calendar requires `calendarCheckedAt` within the previous 24 hours and `calendarWindow` covering the whole proposal. Missing, future, stale or partial sync is unresolved. Unavailable calendar status is unresolved, and already known busy intervals still block overlap. Manual availability with no connection supports the complete workflow.
- TRAVEL-01: A firm travel limit requires an explicit nonblank origin, in addition to current per-participant evidence for minutes.
- BOUNDS-01: Availability, busy and preference arrays contain at most 64 items; dietary arrays at most 32. Setup window spans at most 31 days and dinner duration at most 480 minutes.
- FEASIBILITY-01: Every required participant needs explicit availability covering the whole dinner. Missing price, location, opening, hard dietary or hard travel evidence makes a proposal unresolved. Contradictory evidence, future retrieval times and claims older than 48 hours are unresolved. Every claim requires its own supporting excerpt and public HTTPS source without credentials or IP literals. Venue city and area must match the agreed search bounds. Allergy accommodation additionally requires a separate staff report from every affected participant; research cannot create these reports. Replacing research invalidates those reports.
- FEASIBILITY-02: Busy overlaps, excessive firm budget or travel, unsupported diet, closed venues, duration mismatches and times outside the setup window make a proposal infeasible. Flexible constraints do not prevent feasibility. Reservation status remains separate from feasibility; confirmed booking is never inferred from agreement.
- PROPOSAL-01: A proposal contains one or two options and recommends index zero. Each version snapshots setup, constraints, required attendees, venue evidence and prices. Organizer selection of an alternative creates a new immutable history entry.
- AGREEMENT-01: Every required attendee must explicitly confirm the same current feasible version. Membership, setup, constraint, evidence or option changes clear confirmations. Stale confirmation is rejected. An unresolved dispute invalidates the active version. Updates cease when the most recent dinner start is reached.
- DISPUTE-01: Every open objection blocks confirmation until its author explicitly resolves it. Each revised proposal increments attempts on every open objection. Two revision attempts are allowed; another objection after two attempts or a third attempted revision escalates. Repeated objections alone do not count as revision attempts. Escalation cannot be erased by generating another proposal or resolving an objection.
- REMINDER-01: Reminders require opt-in, no previous send on the same local calendar date, and local time from 09:00 inclusive to 21:00 exclusive. Agreement ends reminder eligibility. A delivery adapter must atomically reserve a reminder before sending to prevent concurrent duplicate sends.
- ICS-01: Calendar export requires agreement. It includes UTC start and end (including DST conversion), stable UID, proposal sequence, escaped and byte-folded text, location, per-person price and currency, intended required attendee IDs, timezone and actual reservation state. Export itself creates no external calendar event.

## API

Schemas and inferred types live in `src/domain/index.ts`. `createDinner` initializes an aggregate after the application has obtained organizer consent. Organizer commands are `approveMember`, `updateSetup`, `setMemberRequired`, `setVenues`, `proposeOptions` and `selectAlternative`. Participant commands are `updateConstraints`, `setReminderConsent`, `confirmVersion`, `raiseDispute`, `resolveDispute` and `reportAllergySafety`. `evaluateFeasibility`, `reminderEligibility` and `exportIcs` derive results without mutation. Commands and feasibility checks take an explicit `now` ISO instant where time affects behavior. Every state mutation increments aggregate revision, independently of the proposal version, for optimistic persistence. `DomainError.code` distinguishes authorization, membership, stale version, started dinner, feasibility and escalation failures; malformed payloads produce Zod errors.

Research claims each carry a source URL, supporting excerpt, retrieval instant and known/unknown/contradictory status. Dietary evidence is keyed by exact requirement and travel evidence by participant ID. Source URL validation prevents obvious unsafe URL forms; an HTTP research adapter must additionally defend against DNS resolution to private addresses and redirects. Reservation claims cannot assert booked in version one. The internal feasibility reasons contain participant IDs and are private; public summaries must use a privacy projection in the application layer.

Claims may retain contradictory citations as `relatedSources` entries containing URL, excerpt and retrieval timestamp. An allergy staff report requires `accommodationConfirmed: true` from the affected participant's explicit affirmative interaction; free text alone is insufficient. The application must stage the report and request that affirmation before invoking `reportAllergySafety`.

An affirmative staff report is private venue evidence for that participant's own allergy. It can resolve a missing website claim, while contradictory evidence or a current known negative claim still blocks the proposal. It does not resolve another person's requirement.
