# Observable acceptance conversations

Each requirement is exercised by named tests in `tests/domain`, `tests/application`, `tests/adapters` and `tests/persistence`.

1. **SET-01 / SET-02** Organizer opens one form, sets shared city/optional area, window, party size 2–6 and per-person budget, reviews editable timezone/currency/duration defaults and consents. Inline errors identify missing fields. A local time in a DST gap/overlap is rejected pending correction or an explicit occurrence choice.
2. **JOIN-01 / AUTH-01** Friend opens a dinner invite, sees privacy terms, actively consents and submits private input once, then waits for organizer approval. Pending answers are excluded from planning and invisible to the organizer. A stranger cannot approve themselves, read state, confirm or reuse another dinner's controls.
3. **CON-01 / CAL-01** Manual willingness remains required even when Google says free. A calendar conflict blocks the candidate. OAuth denial/revocation leaves manual entry usable. Expired/reused state fails; tokens are encrypted; the originating Telegram participant activates staged credentials.
4. **RES-01 / DIET-01 / FEAS-01** Cuisine-only allergy claims, missing price, unknown travel and conflicting sources leave a proposal provisional. A participant's own staff report can resolve their allergy for that venue. Other firm requirements remain unchanged.
5. **PROP-01 / MED-01 / REV-01 / AGR-01** Organizer selects one of two sourced options. A private objection leads to a revised option. Two unsuccessful rounds escalate. A schedule change clears old confirmations; old buttons fail. Every required person must confirm the new feasible version.
6. **FIN-01** Calendar export matches the current agreed instants, duration, venue and sequence; reservation text says link/manual handoff and never claims a booking.
7. **PRIV-01 / PRIV-02 / OPS-01 / OPS-02** A duplicate webhook is a no-op. A crash rolls back state and outbox together. Concurrent confirmation/change cannot agree an obsolete plan. Quiet hours and opt-out suppress reminders; deletion erases pending private payloads; retention sweeps dinner records.
8. **EVAL-01** Misleading participant/web-page instructions cannot authorize a send, expose another person's input, set confirmations or alter membership. Live runs measure structured outcomes and evidence with privacy/auth violations blocking release.

9. **UI-01..06** A two-person mobile flow opens from Telegram, saves a host form and own date selections, shares an invitation, approves a guest, researches and confirms. Signed identity without a private bot session fails. Independent people can save without staling each other; changes to shared settings stale open forms. Pending guests can privately edit staged answers. Replayed actions reproject current authorized state, never deleted history. Old alternative selections fail. Browser fixtures are visibly synthetic and never enter the production bundle.

10. **UI-07 / UI-08** On iPhone 15 WebKit and narrower screens, creating and editing dinner dates and availability keeps every native input inside its container. Dates and time limits stack vertically. A user applies one range to several dates, edits one saved summary, adds a second range on the same day and resolves any DST ambiguity before saving. An unapplied draft cannot disappear on submit. “Budget per person” always matches the submitted price basis; an older group total requires an explicitly entered per-person amount.
11. **CANCEL-01** Organizer reviews the whole-dinner warning and confirms cancellation; guests cannot cancel it. Both collecting and agreed dinners become read-only cancelled records, free the organizer's active-dinner slot and stop queued work. Duplicate requests send no duplicate notice and preserve the original end timestamp. A pending guest sees cancellation rather than waiting for approval, with staged answers erased. Racing confirmation, slow research and delayed OAuth cannot revive the plan. Opt-out after cancellation preserves terminal status. Existing reservations and calendar downloads require manual cleanup.
